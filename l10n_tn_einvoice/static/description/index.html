Seamless Live Workflow

How the module handles your invoicing lifecycle automatically.

1 - Data Validation

Odoo validates fiscal matricules, partner addresses, and tax configurations before generation.

2 - XML Generation

The system generates a TEIF-compliant XML file based on your invoice data and custom templates.

3 - Digital Signature

One-click signing via ANCE/NGSign. The signed XML is stored securely as an attachment.

4 - TTN Submission

The signed invoice is sent to TTN for official validation. Odoo monitors the status until 'Validated'.

5 -QR & Archiving

Retrieval of the official QR code and archived SEAL from TTN. Your invoice is now 100% compliant.
