Tunisian Stamp Tax Module for Odoo 17

Overview

The Tunisian Stamp Tax Module for Odoo 17 is a powerful tool designed to automate the calculation and application of stamp taxes in Tunisian businesses. This module ensures your business stays compliant with local tax regulations while simplifying your financial operations.

Key Features

  • Automatic stamp tax calculation on invoices, orders, and contracts.
  • Configurable tax rates based on Tunisian law.
  • Detailed tax reports for auditing and compliance.
  • Seamless integration with Odoo's accounting and invoicing features.

Installation

Installing the Tunisian Stamp Tax Module is easy and straightforward:

  1. Download the module from the Odoo App Store.
  2. Upload it to your Odoo addons directory.
  3. Navigate to the Apps menu in Odoo and click Update Apps List.
  4. Search for "Tunisian Stamp Tax" and click Install.

Configuration

After installation, configure the stamp tax rates:

  1. Go to Accounting > Configuration > Taxes.
  2. Create a new tax record with the appropriate rate for stamp tax.
  3. Assign the tax to applicable document types like invoices or purchase orders.

Usage

Once configured, the stamp tax will be automatically applied to relevant financial documents. You can view the tax as a separate line item, ensuring clear and transparent transactions. Access detailed reports under the Reporting menu to monitor tax amounts across different transactions.
