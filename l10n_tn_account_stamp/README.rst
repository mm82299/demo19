===========================
Tunisian Stamp Tax Module
===========================

Overview
========

The **Tunisian Stamp Tax Module** for Odoo 17 is designed to automate the calculation and application of stamp taxes in Tunisian businesses. This module helps ensure compliance with local tax regulations and simplifies financial operations within Odoo.

Key Features
============

- Automatic calculation and application of stamp tax on invoices, orders, and contracts.
- Configurable tax rates based on Tunisian tax law.
- Detailed tax reporting for auditing and compliance.
- Seamless integration with Odoo's accounting and invoicing modules.

Installation
============

To install the Tunisian Stamp Tax Module:

1. Download the module from the Odoo App Store or your preferred repository.
2. Place the module in your Odoo addons directory.
3. Update the Odoo App List by navigating to **Apps** > **Update Apps List**.
4. Search for the "Tunisian Stamp Tax" module and click **Install**.

Configuration
=============

Once installed, you can configure the stamp tax rates as follows:

1. Go to **Accounting** > **Configuration** > **Taxes**.
2. Create a new tax record with the appropriate rate for the stamp tax.
3. Assign the tax to relevant document types such as invoices or purchase orders.

Usage
=====

After configuration, the stamp tax will be automatically applied to applicable financial documents. The tax will appear as a separate line item, ensuring clear and transparent transactions.

Reports detailing the stamp tax amounts applied to various transactions can be accessed under the **Reporting** menu in the **Accounting** module.
